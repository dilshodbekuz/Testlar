--- O'RTACHA ---

11. "Birinchi…(-dan, -si, -ga) bilimli, donishmand kishilar, turli kasb egalari bo'lsa" jumlasida nuqtalar o'rniga qaysi qo'shimcha qo'yiladi?

B) -dan
C) -ga
D) -lar

+A) -ga
B) -da
C) -ni
D) Hech qaysi

B) -dan
C) -ning
+D) -ni

B) -mand
C) -dor
+D) -li

15. "Undan mashaqqat tugunlari yech…(-in, -il, -ish)adi" jumlasida to'g'ri qo'shimchani tanlang.

B) -in
+C) -il
D) -ish
