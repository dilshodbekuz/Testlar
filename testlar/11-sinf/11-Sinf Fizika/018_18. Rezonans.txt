18. Rezonans

--- OSON ---

1. O'zgaruvchan tok zanjirida R, L va C ketma-ket ulangan. Rezonans vaqtida zanjirning to'la qarshiligi nimaga teng bo'ladi?
+A) Z = R
B) Z = XL + XC
C) Z = 0
D) Z = XL − XC

2. Ketma-ket ulangan R, L, C zanjirida rezonans yuz berishi uchun qaysi shart bajarilishi kerak?
A) R = XL
+B) XL = XC
C) R = XC
D) XL = 2XC

3. Rezonans chastotasi ω_rez qaysi formula bilan aniqlanadi?
A) ω_rez = LC
+B) ω_rez = 1/√(LC)
C) ω_rez = 1/(LC)
D) ω_rez = √(LC)

4. Zanjirdagi tok kuchi amplitudasi I_m ning tashqi kuchlanish chastotasiga bog'liqlik grafigi nima deb ataladi?
+A) Rezonans egri chizig'i
B) Faza egri chizig'i
C) Garmonik egri chiziq
D) Sinusoida

5. Zanjirdagi rezonans nima uchun kuchlanishlar rezonansi deb ataladi?
A) Chunki aktiv qarshilikdagi kuchlanish nolga tushadi
B) Chunki zanjirga qo'yilgan kuchlanish nolga tenglashadi
+C) Chunki rezonansda g'altak va kondensatordagi kuchlanishlar keskin ortadi
D) Chunki kuchlanish chastotasi o'zgarmaydi

6. Rezonans vaqtida zanjirdagi tok kuchining amplitudasi qaysi formula bilan topiladi?
+A) I_m = U_m/R
B) I_m = U_m·R
C) I_m = U_m·Z
D) I_m = U_m/(XL + XC)

7. Aktiv qarshiligi nolga teng bo'lgan tebranish konturida erkin tebranishlar chastotasi (xususiy chastota) ω0 qanday aniqlanadi?
A) ω0 = √(LC)
+B) ω0 = 1/√(LC)
C) ω0 = L/C
D) ω0 = C/L

8. R, L, C ketma-ket ulangan zanjirning to'la qarshiligi qaysi ifoda bilan aniqlanadi?
A) Z = R + XL + XC
+B) Z = √(R² + (XL − XC)²)
C) Z = R + (XL − XC)
D) Z = √(R² + (XL + XC)²)

9. Zanjirda rezonans vujudga kelishi uchun tashqi davriy kuchlanish chastotasi qanday bo'lishi kerak?
+A) Zanjirning xususiy chastotasiga teng
B) Xususiy chastotadan juda kichik
C) Nolga teng
D) Zanjirning xususiy chastotasidan ikki marta katta

10. Rezonans vaqtida g'altak va kondensatordagi kuchlanish tebranishlari amplitudasi haqida qaysi fikr to'g'ri?
A) U doim tashqi kuchlanishga teng bo'ladi
B) U har doim tashqi kuchlanishdan kichik bo'ladi
+C) U tashqi kuchlanishdan ham katta bo'lishi mumkin
D) U doim nolga teng bo'ladi

--- O'RTACHA ---

11. R = 50 Ω bo'lgan zanjirda rezonans yuz bergan. Kuchlanish amplitudasi U_m = 100 V bo'lsa, tok kuchi amplitudasi qancha?
A) 5 A
+B) 2 A
C) 0,5 A
D) 50 A

12. Zanjirda L = 0,1 H va C = 10 µF (10⁻⁵ F). Rezonans uchun burchak chastota ω_rez qancha?
A) 100 rad/s
B) 10 000 rad/s
+C) 1000 rad/s
D) 31,4 rad/s

13. Ketma-ket zanjirda R = 10 Ω, XL = 30 Ω, XC = 30 Ω, U_m = 50 V. Tok kuchi amplitudasi qancha?
A) 0,83 A
+B) 5 A
C) 2,5 A
D) 1,25 A

14. Rezonans vaqtida XL = XC = 40 Ω va I_m = 0,5 A. G'altakdagi kuchlanish amplitudasi qancha?
A) 80 V
+B) 20 V
C) 40 V
D) 0,0125 V

15. Konturdagi g'altak induktivligi L 4 marta oshirilsa (C o'zgarmasa), rezonans chastotasi qanday o'zgaradi?
A) 2 marta ortadi
B) 4 marta kamayadi
+C) 2 marta kamayadi
D) 4 marta ortadi

16. Konturdagi kondensator sig'imi C 9 marta kamaytirilsa (L o'zgarmasa), rezonans chastotasi qanday o'zgaradi?
+A) 3 marta ortadi
B) 3 marta kamayadi
C) 9 marta ortadi
D) 9 marta kamayadi

17. Aktiv qarshilik R kamaytirilsa, rezonans egri chizig'ining maksimumi qanday o'zgaradi?
A) Yo'qoladi
B) Pasayadi
C) O'zgarmaydi
+D) Balandlashadi

18. Zanjirda R = 20 Ω, XL = 40 Ω, XC = 25 Ω. To'la qarshilik Z qancha?
A) 35 Ω
+B) 25 Ω
C) 15 Ω
D) 85 Ω

19. Zanjirdagi L = 0,2 H. Rezonans chastotasi ω0 = 1000 rad/s bo'lishi uchun kondensator sig'imi qancha bo'lishi kerak?
A) 2·10⁻⁶ F
B) 5·10⁻³ F
C) 0,2·10⁻³ F
+D) 5·10⁻⁶ F

20. Rezonansda U_m = 10 V, R = 5 Ω, L = 0,1 H, C = 10⁻⁵ F. U_L = (U_m/R)·√(L/C) formula bo'yicha g'altakdagi kuchlanish amplitudasi qancha?
A) 100 V
B) 2 V
+C) 200 V
D) 20 V

--- QIYIN ---

21. Ketma-ket zanjirda R = 10 Ω, L = 0,1 H, C = 10⁻⁵ F, U_m = 20 V. Rezonans chastotasida kondensatordagi kuchlanish amplitudasi qancha?

+B) 200 V

+A) 3 marta
B) 9 marta
C) 1 marta (teng)

+D) Har ikki holda ham kichik bo'ladi

B) 100 Hz
C) 707 Hz
D) 450 Hz

25. R = 200 Ω, L = 0,4 H, C = 5·10⁻⁶ F va U_m = 120 V bo'lgan zanjirda manba chastotasi rezonans chastotasiga tenglashtirilsa, tok amplitudasi qancha bo'ladi?
