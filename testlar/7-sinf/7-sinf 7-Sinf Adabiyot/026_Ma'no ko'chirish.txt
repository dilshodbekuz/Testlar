--- QIYIN ---

+C) Istiora

+B) Majozda noaniq tushuncha ko'pchilikka ma'lum narsalarning belgilari bilan ifodalanadi; ramzda mavhum axloqiy-ma'naviy sifat shunga ega narsa-jonivor orqali ifodalanadi
